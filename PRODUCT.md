# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, single page deployed on Vercel (eagle-peru.vercel.app). Public-sector edition; siblings: eagle-junction-2026 (UTP) and eagle-sponsors (brands).

## Users

Primary: Peruvian public institutions (ministries, scholarship, innovation and country-promotion agencies, regional governments) deciding whether to endorse or fund the team's trip from Lima to Junction 2026 in Espoo, Finland. Secondary: the team's LinkedIn network who share the page.

## Product Purpose

Announce team Eagle's run at Junction 2026 (13–15 Nov 2026, Hype Areena, Espoo; ~2,000 builders, 48 h, 100+ countries, €100k prize pool) and get institutional backing through three levels (Auspicio, Viaje, Delegación) or through an existing programme or call.

## Positioning

A UTP team with production and hackathon track record (not first-timers) taking Peru onto the stage that Google DeepMind, NVIDIA, OpenAI and AWS sponsor.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Bilingual ES/EN.
- Team name: Eagle. Members 02–04 are not confirmed yet; placeholders must be labelled as such.
- Acceptance to Junction is not confirmed in the evidence; copy must not claim the team was accepted.
- Junction partner logos may be shown only as event partners, with a visible note that they do not sponsor the team.

## Brand Commitments

Visual essence pinned by the user: the Junction 2026 site (Renaissance paintings remixed with hacker culture, computer-vision detection boxes, dark ground, manifesto voice "move the center of gravity"). Junction's own images are not reused; public-domain originals get the same treatment.

## Product Principles

1. The ask (back the trip) is visible in the first viewport.
2. Peru gets an empty slot next to the event's partners; the institution sees how visible it becomes per level.
3. Proof over adjectives: shipped work with real numbers.
4. The team, not one person.
